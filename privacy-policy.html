Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27th December 2025

At Lord Walker, your privacy matters. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you visit or make a purchase from our website.

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with our website, we may collect the following information:

  • Personal details such as name, email address, phone number, and shipping address
  • Order and payment-related information (processed securely via third-party gateways)
  • Device information such as IP address, browser type, and browsing behavior

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Process and deliver your orders
  • Communicate order updates, support requests, and important service messages
  • Improve our website, products, and customer experience
  • Send promotional communication (only if you opt in)

3. Payment & Transaction Security

We do not store your credit or debit card details. All payments are processed through secure, PCI-compliant third-party payment gateways. Your financial information remains encrypted and protected.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or trade your personal data. Your information may only be shared with:

  • Trusted service partners (shipping, logistics, payment processing)
  • Legal or regulatory authorities, if required by law

5. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to enhance site functionality, analyze traffic, and personalize your experience. You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings.

6. Data Security

We implement reasonable security practices and procedures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure. However, no online transmission is 100% secure.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access or update your personal information
  • Withdraw consent for marketing communication
  • Request deletion of your data, subject to legal obligations

8. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party sites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of such websites.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
