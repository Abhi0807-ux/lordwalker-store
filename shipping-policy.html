Shipping Policy

Last updated: 27th December 2025

At Lord Walker, we aim to deliver your order as quickly and smoothly as possible. Below is an overview of how our shipping process works.

Order Processing Time

Most orders are processed and shipped within 1 business day after order confirmation. However, certain styles or variants may require additional procurement time. In such cases, order processing may take up to 4–5 business days. Customers will be notified in case of any extended processing timelines.

Shipping Partners

We work with reputed and reliable courier partners to ensure safe and priority-based delivery. Our logistics partners are selected for their reach, reliability, and service quality.

Delivery Timelines

Once shipped, delivery timelines typically depend on your location and courier operations. While most shipments are delivered on priority, some deliveries may take additional time due to:

  • Remote or non-serviceable locations
  • Courier network delays
  • Weather conditions, strikes, or unforeseen circumstances

Delivery timelines provided at checkout are estimates and not guaranteed.

Tracking Information

Once your order is shipped, tracking details will be shared via email or SMS to help you monitor your delivery status.

Shipping Charges

Shipping charges, if applicable, will be clearly displayed at checkout before payment. Any promotional free-shipping offers will be mentioned on the website.

Delays & Liability

While we make every effort to ensure timely delivery, Lord Walker shall not be held responsible for delays caused by courier partners or factors beyond our control. In the rare event of a shipment being delayed or misplaced, our support team will assist you in coordinating with the courier partner for resolution.
