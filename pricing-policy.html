Pricing Policy

Last updated: 27th December 2025

At Lord Walker, we aim to keep our pricing fair, transparent, and easy to understand. Prices reflect the quality of materials, craftsmanship, and care that go into every product.

Base Pricing & Currency Display

All product prices are originally set in Indian Rupees (INR). For customer convenience, prices may be displayed in multiple currencies using automated currency conversion tools. Prices shown in currencies other than INR are approximate and may vary slightly due to exchange rate fluctuations applied by payment gateways or issuing banks.

Final Transaction Amount

The final payable amount is determined at checkout and processed in the selected currency, subject to foreign exchange rates, international transaction fees, or charges applied by the payment service provider or bank. Lord Walker is not responsible for any difference arising due to currency conversion, foreign exchange charges, or bank-imposed fees.

Customs Duties & Local Taxes

Product prices displayed on the website do not include any local customs duties, import taxes, VAT, GST, or other charges applicable in the recipient's country. Such charges, if levied by local authorities, are the responsibility of the customer and must be paid directly to the courier or customs authority at the time of delivery.

Offers, Discounts & Promotions

Promotional pricing, discounts, or offers may be available from time to time and may vary across regions or currencies. These offers:

  • Are valid for a limited period only
  • Cannot be combined unless explicitly stated

Discounts cannot be applied retroactively to previously placed orders.

Pricing Errors

In the event of a pricing or currency conversion error, Lord Walker reserves the right to cancel the affected order and issue a full refund.

Final Pricing Authority

The price confirmed at checkout shall be considered final. No claims related to pricing differences after order placement will be entertained.
